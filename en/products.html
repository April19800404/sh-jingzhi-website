Tooling & Process Equipment

Dies · Fixtures · Gauges · Inductors

Tooling and equipment retrofit for forming, machining, locating, measuring and testing — including the Shanghai Manufacturing Single Champion product "automotive driveshaft tooling dies".

  • Dies & forming tools — warm forging, cold extrusion, rotary swaging and cold sizing dies for driveshaft ball cages and splines.
  • Design & simulation — 2D/3D product analysis, step modeling, forming simulation, load & stress analysis, material and coating selection.
  • Measuring, locating & testing — coaxiality and indexing gauges, electronic gauges; machining fixtures, check fixtures and fatigue/vibration test fixtures.
  • Machining, inspection & retrofit — CNC turning, multi-axis machining, wire EDM; CMM, vision, spectroscopy, metallography; press frame repair and feeder retrofit.
Intelligent Equipment

Assembly · Testing · Traceability · Integration

Assembly, testing, traceability and line integration for automotive driveline and robot joint modules — mechanics, electrics, inspection, control and data acquisition engineered together.

  • Driveline equipment — CVJ assembly & test and differential automatic assembly lines; pressing, greasing, banding, sorting, eddy-current/thread/spline testing stations.
  • Robot joint module equipment — motor, reducer and module assembly with material handling, cleaning, pressing, tightening, testing, changeover and RFID traceability.
  • New energy machines — battery module, PACK assembly and storage equipment: scanning, OCV, plasma cleaning, AOI, coating.
  • Assembly process modules — servo pressing, flexible tightening, metered dispensing, elastic component assembly with process curves and step records.
Smart Chassis

Assembly & test for steering, braking, suspension

Assembly & test equipment for wire-controlled chassis, with emphasis on process data storage, traceability and fast changeover.

  • EPS assembly & test lines — C-EPS, P-EPS, DP-EPS, R-EPS with flexible assembly, data acquisition, MSA, calibration and error-proofing.
  • Integrated brake-by-wire lines — process data storage, traceability, parameter recipes and rapid changeover.
  • Wire-controlled suspension — air supply units and suspension control system assembly & test.
Precision Metal Manufacturing

From prototypes to series production

Precision components and production lines for automotive driveline scenarios — metal series parts, gears and drive assemblies, housings and related assemblies, extending toward brake-by-wire and suspension system assemblies.

  • Full-process control — structure, material, heat treatment, dimensional accuracy, cleanliness and inspection specified item by item.
  • Clear delivery boundaries — prototype, series production and traceability scope confirmed at the proposal stage.
  • System assurance — IATF 16949 / ISO 9001 certified across multiple plants and entities.
Polymer Technology

Precision gears · Housings · Drive assemblies

Precision gears, worms, housings, valve cores and drive assemblies — R&D and manufacturing linked end to end, from design simulation to injection series production.

  • Automotive applications — sunroof actuator worms, active grille shutter actuators, HVAC outlet actuators, water valve gear sets, shifter/parking actuator housings and worms.
  • More scenarios — EPB housings and drive assemblies, flush door handle drive systems, charge port actuators, anti-release lock assemblies.
  • R&D capability — gear and gearbox design, strength/durability analysis, mold flow analysis, NVH simulation, material database.
  • Manufacturing chain — mold making, injection molding, part assembly, laser welding, inspection and validation.
Thermal Management

Structural parts · Heatsinks · Design & manufacturing

Structural parts and heatsinks for telecom, new energy and consumer electronics — the business is extending from telecom into energy storage.

  • Product range — telecom structural parts, heatsinks of all types, liquid-cooling heatsinks, thermal boxes, die-cast structures, cooling for NEV and PV inverters.
  • Process chain — die-cast gating design & simulation, aluminum heat treatment, gear/heat-pipe embedding, dispensing, cleaning, welding, final assembly.
  • Verification — dimensional, X-ray, thermal resistance and thermal tests, salt spray and tensile tests.
Supply Chain Digitalization

Connecting orders, materials, production and delivery

Links order planning, raw materials, production, warehousing and delivery information — business processes and data sources are mapped first, then system interfaces and scope.

  • System integration — data interfaces with ERP, MES, PLM, WMS and equipment, covering work orders, BOM, routings, inventory, labor reports and machine status.
  • Mature interfaces — API and intermediate-table options.
  • Implementation path — data preparation, business onboarding, training, algorithm validation, model tuning, go-live support and acceptance.
