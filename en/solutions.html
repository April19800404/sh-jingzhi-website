Driveline · Steering · Braking · Suspension
Automotive & Components

Covering the key processes of vehicle manufacturing

From a single part's die and gauge to assembly & test lines, drive components and scheduling tools — combined to match driveline, steering, braking, suspension, thermal management and body actuator needs.

  • Differential automatic assembly lines — torque, angle and shim measurement with program-based and physical error-proofing.
  • EPS assembly & test lines — C-EPS, P-EPS, DP-EPS and R-EPS with flexible assembly, calibration and data acquisition.
  • Driveshaft dies and gauges — warm forging, cold extrusion and rotary swaging dies, electronic gauges, integrated check fixtures.
  • Polymer actuator assemblies — gear sets and housings for sunroof, grille, water valve, shifter and parking actuators.
Telecom · Storage · PV
Telecom & New Energy

One-stop from thermal design to manufacturing

Thermal management covers telecom structures and cooling, now extending into energy storage; new energy equipment covers battery modules, PACK assembly and storage dedicated machines.

  • Telecom cooling products — heatsink assemblies, thermal boxes and shielding covers matched to equipment structure and heat requirements.
  • Liquid cooling & die-cast structures — die casting, heat-pipe embedding, dispensing, cleaning, welding and final assembly.
  • Battery module & PACK machines — scanning, OCV test, plasma cleaning, AOI inspection and coating.
  • Reliability verification — X-ray, thermal resistance and thermal tests, salt spray and tensile tests.

Robotics Manufacturing

  • High-precision polymer components — for robotics applications, sold and in series delivery.
