Core Technologies · NATIVE FACTORY AI

The first "Native Factory Industrial AI"

Most industrial AI comes from algorithm companies that barely understand the shop floor. Jingzhi's AI is different — it grew up inside our own factories: 20 years of equipment and manufacturing experience taught it process, machines and takt time from day one.

Our equipment business supplies products, processes and shop-floor expertise, while our industrial AI supplies software tools around production planning, engineering data and execution information. They share the same origin and iterate in a closed loop. This is not "adding AI to a factory" — it is AI that grew out of one.
