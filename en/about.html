About Jingzhi

Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Shanghai, Shanghai Jingzhi Industrial Co., Ltd. builds on manufacturing engineering services to deliver equipment, precision components, thermal management products and industrial AI services for automotive, telecom, new energy and robotics manufacturing.

Company Profile

From manufacturing engineering to industrial AI

Our 2026 interim report summarizes the main business as advanced equipment, precision components, thermal management and industrial AI — two connected businesses: manufacturing supplies products, processes and shop-floor expertise, while industrial AI supplies software tools around production plans, engineering data and execution information.

We started with tooling, dies, gauges, fixtures and inductors; grew into automation, testing and logistics equipment; developed precision injection-molded components and manufacturing management systems; and extended into telecom structures and cooling, automotive series production, assembly & test lines and supply chain digitalization — out of which our Native Factory Industrial AI was incubated.
