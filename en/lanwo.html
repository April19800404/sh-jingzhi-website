Why It Matters

Native Factory AI vs Generic AI

Both call themselves "industrial AI" — where they were born decides whether they understand the factory

Native vs Generic

The common problems of generic AI

  • Born from algorithms, lacking real manufacturing data and constraints
  • Understanding of process, machines and takt time stays on paper
  • Long on-site learning curves; results depend on external data
  • "Bolt-on" deployments disconnected from existing systems

The Native Factory AI way

  • 20 years of equipment and manufacturing — algorithms refined on real lines
  • Trained jointly by manufacturing constraints, process knowledge and field data
  • From scheduling to monitoring — tools that serve the production line directly
  • Same origin as our equipment and components business — closed-loop iteration
Three Agent Modules

AI around the core of manufacturing

Covering quotation, process planning, scheduling, execution, quality and delivery — scoped to your existing systems and data readiness

Three Modules
MODULE 01 · CAPACITY SCHEDULING

Clearer planning constraints and schedules

Around people, machines, materials, processes and due dates — factory modeling, strategy selection, plan generation, rush-order simulation, exception alerts and progress views.

  • Strategy mix — balancing, due-date and material-readiness strategies for high-mix, low-volume, frequent-changeover production.
  • Rush-order simulation — compare scenarios and see the impact on due dates and machine load at a glance.
  • Coordinated production — production, materials and shop floor aligned; predictable work order due dates.
Execution monitoring demo
MODULE 02 · EXECUTION MONITORING

A transparent, controllable shop floor

Around shop-floor operations, events, labor reports and inspections — vision-assisted recording and analysis makes floor status visible and problems traceable.

  • Vision recognition — machine vision and multimodal techniques for floor state recognition and operation records.
  • Events & labor reports — operations, events, reports and inspections presented and analyzed together.
  • Scenario-based validation — recognition scope and performance verified against station environment, capture conditions and data management needs.
MODULE 03 · PROCESS OPTIMIZATION

Process know-how, organized and reusable

Around drawings and knowledge bases, the agent helps process engineers organize and reuse expertise — outputs always pass engineering review; engineers stay the decision-makers.

  • Drawing parsing — dimensions, geometric tolerances, surface roughness, machining and inspection requirements.
  • Key parameter extraction — generative models for understanding process documents and assisted route generation.
  • Similar-process retrieval — historical process data structured, retained and reused under engineering review.
